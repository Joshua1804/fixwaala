# Verdant Home UI Design Guidelines

## Color Palette

  Role               Hex
  ------------------ -----------
  Primary            `#0F2515`
  Primary Hover      `#183321`
  Secondary (Sage)   `#8FAF93`
  Accent (Gold)      `#C8A96A`
  Background         `#F2F0EF`
  Surface            `#FFFFFF`
  Card               `#FBF8F5`
  Divider            `#D8D4D1`
  Text Primary       `#0F2515`
  Text Secondary     `#666D69`
  Disabled           `#B8B8B8`

## Background

-   App background: `#F2F0EF`
-   Use white (`#FFFFFF`) only for cards, dialogs, search bars, and
    bottom sheets.

## Top Navigation

-   Background: `#0F2515`
-   Logo & icons: White
-   Notification badge: `#C85A5A`

## Search Bar

-   Background: White
-   Search icon: `#8FAF93`
-   Placeholder: `#8C8C8C`
-   Input text: `#0F2515`

## Hero Banner

-   Overlay: `rgba(15,37,21,0.55)` gradient
-   Heading: White
-   Subtitle: White (90%)
-   CTA: Background `#0F2515`, text White, pressed `#183321`

## Category Icons

### Default

-   Background: `#E6F0E7`
-   Border: `#8FAF93`
-   Icon: `#355E3B`
-   Label: `#0F2515`

### Selected

-   Background: `#0F2515`
-   Icon: White

## Service Cards

-   Background: White
-   Border: `#E6E2DE`
-   Title: `#0F2515`
-   Description: `#666D69`
-   Price: `#0F2515`
-   Rating stars: `#C8A96A`
-   Primary button: `#0F2515` with white text

## Buttons

### Primary

-   Background: `#0F2515`
-   Text: White
-   Pressed: `#183321`

### Secondary

-   Background: White
-   Border/Text: `#0F2515`

### Tertiary

-   Text only: `#355E3B`

## Bottom Navigation

-   Background: White
-   Active icon/text: `#0F2515`
-   Inactive: `#8FAF93`
-   Divider: `#D8D4D1`

## Offer Cards

-   Background: `#5D7A5D`
-   Text: White
-   Badge: `#C8A96A`

## Status Colors

-   Success: `#3D8B5E`
-   Warning: `#D8A441`
-   Error: `#C85A5A`
-   Info: `#4C8AB8`

## Typography

-   Heading: `#0F2515`
-   Body: `#666D69`
-   Caption: `#8C8C8C`
-   Disabled: `#B8B8B8`

## Borders & Icons

-   Card border: `#E5E2DE`
-   Input border: `#D8D4D1`
-   Focus: `#8FAF93`
-   Selected: `#0F2515`

## Chips

-   Available: Background `#E6F0E7`, Text `#355E3B`
-   Popular: Background `#FFF5E2`, Text `#C8A96A`
-   Premium: Background `#0F2515`, Text White

## Layout

-   16 px card radius
-   12--14 px button radius
-   56--64 px category icons
-   24--32 px section spacing
-   16 px grid spacing
-   Soft shadows (5--8% opacity)

## 60--30--10 Rule

-   60% Background: `#F2F0EF`
-   30% Primary Greens
-   10% Accent (`#C8A96A` / `#8FAF93`)
